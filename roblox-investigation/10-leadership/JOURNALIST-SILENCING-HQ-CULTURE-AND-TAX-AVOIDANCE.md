## 3. Executive Tax Avoidance: *The New York Times* QSBS Investigation

In December 2021, *The New York Times* revealed that CEO David Baszucki utilized the **Qualified Small Business Stock (QSBS)** tax exemption (Section 1202) to shield tens of millions of dollars in capital gains from federal taxation:
* **The "Stacking" Technique:** Baszucki gifted pre-IPO Roblox shares to his wife, children, and mother-in-law, multiplying the standard $10 million tax-free capital gains threshold at least **12 times**.
* The strategy drew sharp condemnation from economists and lawmakers for abusing a tax incentive designed for small startups to benefit a multi-billion-dollar tech executive.

## 4. Deceptive Youth Advertising: TINA.org FTC Complaint (April 2022)

Advertising watchdog *Truth in Advertising* (TINA.org) filed a formal FTC complaint alleging:
1. **Undisclosed "Advergames":** Branded promotional experiences (Nike, Walmart, Netflix) seamlessly blended with gameplay without clear disclosures for children under 13.
2. **AI Avatar Influencers:** Unlabeled promotional bots manipulating youth into purchasing Robux.
3. **Misleading Earning Claims:** Deceptively enticing children to develop games with unrealistic promises of wealth.
