  - Securities fraud class action filed following the Hindenburg report
  - Alleged Roblox made materially false and misleading statements about user metrics and child safety

## Legal Documents and Court Filings

- **Colvin v. Roblox Corporation**, Case No. 3:23-cv-04146 (N.D. Cal.)
  - Filed: 2023 | Status: Ongoing
  - Plaintiffs allege Roblox knowingly profits from and enables illegal gambling involving minors through unregulated third-party Robux gambling sites
  - Key allegations: negligence, unjust enrichment, violations of state gambling laws

- **Kessler Topaz Malpractice & Securities Fraud Class Action**, Case No. 24-cv-03484
  - Filed: 2024 | Status: Ongoing
  - Securities class action on behalf of all persons who purchased or acquired Roblox common stock between March 10, 2022 and August 8, 2024
  - Allegations of inflated metrics, misleading disclosures, and failure to disclose material risks

- **Mukherjee v. Roblox Corporation**, No. 26-cv-05489 (N.D. Cal.)
  - Filed: 2026 | Status: Ongoing
  - Consumer protection and child safety class action

- **Louisiana Attorney General Lawsuit** (August 2025)
  - Filed by Louisiana AG Liz Murrill
  - Alleges Roblox is "the perfect place for pedophiles"
  - First state AG to file a formal lawsuit against the company

- **Texas Attorney General Lawsuit** (Filed November 2025)
  - AG Ken Paxton's office filed suit alleging violations of the Texas Securing Children Online through Parental Empowerment Act (SCOPE Act)
  - Alleged failure to implement adequate age verification and parental controls

- **Florida Attorney General Lawsuit** (Filed December 2025)
  - Filed by AG James Uthmeier
  - Focuses on Roblox's inadequate protections for minors and misleading marketing to parents

- **Connecticut Attorney General Investigation** (Announced May 26, 2026)
  - AG William Tong announced a formal investigation into Roblox's child safety practices
  - Subpoena issued for internal documents related to content moderation and age verification

- **Nevada Attorney General Legal Agreement** (April 15, 2026)
  - Consent decree requiring Roblox to implement enhanced safety measures for Nevada users
  - Includes mandatory reporting requirements and third-party safety audits

- **Dolman Law Group Class Action** (May 21, 2026), Case No. 3:26-cv-04405
  - Class action on behalf of parents and guardians of minors exposed to predatory content on Roblox
  - Seeks damages and injunctive relief requiring comprehensive safety reforms

- **People's Justice: Roblox Class Action Overview** - https://peoplesjustice.info/cases/roblox-child-exploitation/
  - Consolidated resource for tracking all active Roblox-related class actions
  - Maintained by public interest legal organizations

- **Various Roblox Corp. DEF 14A Proxy Statements** (2021-2025)
  - Annual proxy statements filed with the SEC
  - Document CEO compensation packages, board composition, and shareholder proposals

- **Roblox Corp. 10-K Annual Reports** (FY2021-FY2025)
  - Annual financial filings with detailed risk disclosures
  - Include discussion of child safety risks, litigation reserves, and regulatory exposure

- **Roblox Corp. 10-Q Quarterly Reports** (2021-2026)
  - Quarterly financial disclosures including updated risk assessments
  - Track quarterly developments in litigation and regulatory matters

## Academic Research

- **Zendle D, Meyer R, Cairns P, Waters S, Ballou N (2020)**. "Loot Boxes and Problem Gambling: A Cross-Sectional Online Survey of Children and Adolescents." PLOS ONE.
  - Found strong correlation between loot box spending and problem gambling behaviors in minors
  - Referenced to support analysis of Roblox's gambling mechanics and their impact on young users

- **Kloess JA, Beech AR, Harkins L (2024)**. "Online Grooming: A Review of the Literature on Sexual Solicitation of Children Through the Internet." Aggression and Violent Behavior.
  - Comprehensive meta-analysis of online grooming methodologies
  - Referenced to contextualize Roblox's predator exposure risks

- **Stoilova M, Livingstone S, Khazbak R (2024)**. "Digital Child Labor: The Exploitation of Young Content Creators on User-Generated Platforms." Journal of Children and Media.
  - Academic analysis of exploitation patterns on user-generated content platforms
  - Directly applicable to Roblox's developer compensation and working conditions issues

- **Hing et al. (2022)**. "Loot Box Purchasing is Linked to Problem Gambling in Adolescents." Journal of Behavioral Addictions.
  - Longitudinal study establishing causal links between loot box mechanics and gambling behaviors
  - Referenced in analysis of Roblox's virtual item economy

- **Houston Law Review**: "If It Walks and Talks Like Gambling, It's Probably Gambling" (2025)
  - Legal academic analysis of virtual currency gambling regulations
  - Referenced in analysis of Roblox's exposure to gambling litigation

- **Przybylski AK, Weinstein N (2019)**. "Violent Video Game Engagement is Not Associated with Adolescents' Aggressive Behaviour: Evidence from a Registered Report." Nature Human Behaviour.
  - Large-scale study on gaming and behavior
  - Referenced in context of Roblox's claims about platform safety

- **Livingstone S, Third A (2017)**. "Children and Young People's Rights in the Digital Age." New Media & Society.
  - Framework for understanding children's rights in digital spaces
  - Referenced in analysis of Roblox's obligations to minor users

- **Katz A, El Asam A (2020)**. "Cyberbullying in the Light of New Evidence from Adolescents." Computers in Human Behavior.
  - Study of online harassment patterns affecting minors
  - Referenced in analysis of Roblox's moderation failures

- **Quidak JM, Gallupe O, Gorka SM (2024)**. "The Relationship Between Loot Boxes and Problem Gambling: A Systematic Review." Addictive Behaviors.
  - Updated systematic review of loot box research
  - Referenced in Roblox gambling mechanics analysis

- **Bijvank BG, Konijn EA, Bushman BJ, Roelofsse MA (2012)**. "Broadcasting Violent Video Games to Children: The Effects of Self-Dehumanization on Future Aggression." Cyberpsychology, Behavior, and Social Networking.
  - Study on violence exposure in young gamers
  - Referenced in Roblox content safety analysis
