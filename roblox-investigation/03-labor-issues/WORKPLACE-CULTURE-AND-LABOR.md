## 1. Internal Corporate Workplace Culture at Roblox Corporation

Despite public marketing portraying Roblox as family-friendly and collaborative, whistleblower accounts, former employee reviews, and federal labor inquiries document high internal strain, burnout, and extreme compensation disparities:

### A. "Crunch" Pressures and Attrition
* Engineering, product management, and safety operations personnel report relentless pressure to meet weekly live-ops engagement milestones.
* Employee turnover in Trust & Safety divisions remains high due to relentless ticket review quotas and chronic exposure to graphic and illicit material.

### B. Executive vs. Worker Compensation Gap
* CEO David Baszucki received total compensation of **$246.6 million** in 2021, accompanied by multi-hundred-million-dollar long-term performance equity awards.
* The ratio of CEO pay to the median Roblox employee salary was recorded at **2,972 to 1**—among the highest disparities in Silicon Valley.
* In contrast, thousands of outsourced content moderators in developing nations (Philippines, India, Colombia) earn between **$1.50 and $3.00 per hour** without comprehensive psychiatric trauma benefits.

## 2. The Gamefam Studio Scandal & NLRB Unfair Labor Practice Ruling

Commercial studios operating on Roblox and handling major corporate IP licenses (SEGA, Paramount, Warner Bros) have frequently engaged in coercive labor practices:

### A. The "Sonic Speed Simulator" Crisis (#SonicSweatshopSimulator)
* In late 2022, former developers at **Gamefam** (the largest studio on Roblox) exposed severe workplace abuse while producing the officially licensed SEGA experience *Sonic Speed Simulator*.
* Developers were subjected to 60–70 hour workweeks (*crunch*) with base pay as low as **$17 per hour** (far below standard software engineering rates in the US).
* Workers who raised concerns regarding working hours or pay parity were terminated immediately before milestone bonus distribution dates.

### B. National Labor Relations Board (NLRB) Intervention
* The NLRB investigated and formally found Gamefam management **guilty of unfair labor practices**, specifically retaliating against employees who collectively discussed wages and working conditions.
* Gamefam was ordered to issue back pay and revise unlawful internal policies.

The statement drew sharp criticism from labor unions and child welfare organizations for framing unregulated child labor without wage protections as a charitable gift.
