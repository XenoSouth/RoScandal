## 3. Average Bookings Per Daily Active User (ABPDAU) Distortion

$$\text{ABPDAU}_{\text{Reported}} = \frac{\text{Quarterly Bookings}}{\text{Reported DAU}} = \frac{\$950\text{M}}{88.9\text{M}} = \mathbf{\$10.68\text{ per reported account}}$$

$$\text{ABPDAU}_{\text{True Human}} = \frac{\text{Quarterly Bookings}}{\text{Real Human DAU}} = \frac{\$950\text{M}}{58.0\text{M}} = \mathbf{\$16.38\text{ per real human}}$$

* **Key Finding:** Reporting 88.9M DAU artificially suppresses apparent monetization per user, masking a collapsing real conversion rate (<2.5%–3.5% paying users) and extreme dependence on minor "whale" spenders.
