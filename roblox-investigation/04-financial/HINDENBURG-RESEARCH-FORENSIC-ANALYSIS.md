## 1. Executive Summary

On October 8, 2024, renowned forensic financial research and short-selling firm **Hindenburg Research** released a comprehensive investigative report regarding Roblox Corporation. The report alleged a dual systemic fraud:
1. **Wall Street Metrics Deception:** Artificially inflating Daily Active Users (DAU) by **25% to 42%+** and engagement hours by **100%+** through intentional conflation of alternative accounts (*alts*) with unique human beings and the uninhibited operation of automated bot farms.
2. **Safety Budget Cuts to Boost Margins:** Deprioritizing child safety by cutting the Trust & Safety operational budget by ~2% (Q2 2024) to show operating leverage and satisfy Wall Street profitability targets.
3. **Insider Enrichment:** Senior executives and corporate insiders liquidated over **$1.7 billion** in RBLX stock since the direct listing (IPO) in March 2021, with CEO David Baszucki personally cashing out over **$115 million** within a 12-month period while inflated metrics were reported.

### A. Account Conflation vs. Unique Humans
In correspondence with the SEC, Roblox claimed it could not determine whether individual users maintained multiple accounts. However, testimonies from former internal Roblox data engineers and software architects demonstrated:
* Roblox possesses advanced telemetry capable of hardware fingerprinting, IP clustering, and browser session tracking.
* Management deliberately reported unfiltered metrics (*non-de-alted data*) in quarterly SEC filings (10-Q/10-K) to maintain the narrative of exponential user growth.

### B. Industrial Bot Farms (Southeast Asia & Low-Cost Regions)
* Forensic investigations identified widespread bot farms operating 24/7 in Vietnam, the Philippines, and Indonesia.
* These automated emulators engage in Robux farming, experience ranking manipulation, and asset scraping.
* Because bots remain active continuously, they massively distort both daily active user counts and aggregate platform engagement hours.

## 3. Engagement Hours Distortion & Ad Impression Fraud

### A. 2.4 Hours vs. 22 Minutes Daily Engagement
* **Roblox Official Claim:** Reported **17.4 billion hours of engagement** in Q2 2024, representing an average of **2.4 hours per DAU daily**—surpassing YouTube and TikTok.
* **Third-Party Telemetry Data:** Independent mobile tracking data (*Sensor Tower* and *Apptopia*) revealed that actual human mobile app engagement averaged only **22 to 30 minutes per day**.
* **Distortion Drivers:**
  1. *AFK (Away From Keyboard) Farming:* Games designed to incentivize players to leave computers and phones running overnight.
  2. *Continuous Bot Activity:* Millions of scripted accounts logged in around the clock.

### B. Impact on Fortune 500 Brand Advertisers
Roblox aggressively markets its platform to global brands (Nike, Walmart, Gucci, Hyundai, Disney). If a substantial fraction of impressions and engagement hours are generated by bot clusters and duplicate alts:
* Advertisers are paying premium rates for phantom impressions.
* Average Bookings Per Daily Active User (ABPDAU) metrics become fundamentally misleading.

## 6. Regulatory & Legal Fallout (2024–2026)

1. **Securities Fraud Class Actions:** Shareholder law firms (including *Kessler Topaz Meltzer & Check LLP v. Roblox Corp*) filed federal class actions in the Northern District of California under the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 for metric misrepresentation.
2. **Emergency Policy Overhaul (November 2024):** Roblox restricted direct messaging for users under 13 and deployed new parental control interfaces to mitigate regulatory pressure.
3. **State AG Litigation:** Findings from the Hindenburg report were cited in deceptive trade practice complaints by State Attorneys General across Arkansas, Louisiana, Texas, and Florida.
