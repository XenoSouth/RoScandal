### A. European Commission Digital Services Act (DSA) Investigation (January 2026)
* The Dutch Authority for Consumers and Markets (ACM) and the European Commission initiated formal proceedings under **Article 28 of the DSA** regarding systemic risks to children, online grooming, exposure to harmful content, and deceptive purchasing loops.
* Violations carry financial penalties of up to **6% of Roblox's annual global turnover**.

### B. UK Online Safety Act (OSA) Enforcement (March 2026)
* Ofcom and the ICO issued mandatory transparency notices under the *Online Safety Act 2023* and the *Age Appropriate Design Code*, auditing Roblox's age-verification efficacy, algorithmic recommendation systems, and youth profiling.

### C. Official 16+ Rating Increase in Germany (January 2025)
* Software rating board **USK** officially increased Roblox's classification to **16+** due to unmitigated grooming risks, unmoderated UGC violence, and gambling-adjacent monetization mechanics.

## 2. Middle East Bans & Asia-Pacific Oversight

### A. Nationwide Ban in Turkey (Law No. 5651)
* **Date:** August 7, 2024 (Adana 6th Criminal Court of Peace & BTK).
* **Rationale:** Pervasive child sexual abuse material (CSAM), bot-driven pedophile grooming networks, and exploitation of virtual currency.

### B. Full Access Bans in Iraq, Qatar, and Oman (2025)
* **Iraq (Late 2025):** The Communications and Media Commission (CMC) enacted a permanent national ban citing severe child cyber-extortion risks.
* **Qatar & Oman:** Enacted network-level access restrictions over child welfare and moderation failures.

### C. Termination of China Joint Venture "LuoBuLeSi" (December 2021)
* The localized platform developed with Tencent was permanently shut down after failing to comply with strict youth playtime restrictions (1 hour on weekends) and Chinese state UGC censorship mandates.

### D. Australian eSafety Mandatory Transparency Notices (2026)
* The Australian eSafety Commissioner initiated formal compliance audits under the *Online Safety Act 2021*, with statutory civil penalties of up to **AUD $825,000 per day** or **AUD $49.5 million** for serious non-compliance.

## 3. Latin America: Brazil (ECA Digital Enforcement)

* **Enforcement Agencies:** Senacon (Ministry of Justice), Procon-SP, and Ministério Público Federal (MPF).
* **Statute:** Full implementation of **ECA Digital (Lei nº 15.211/2025)** as of March 2026.
* **Mandatory Actions:**
  * Strict prohibition of paid randomized loot boxes for users under 18.
  * Mandatory automated deactivation of direct chat for children under age 9.
  * Fines of up to **R$ 13 million BRL** per infraction under the Consumer Defense Code.
