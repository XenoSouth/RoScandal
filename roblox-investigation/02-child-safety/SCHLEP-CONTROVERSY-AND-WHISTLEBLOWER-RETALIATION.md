## 2. Background: Schlep's Methodology & The 6 Predator Arrests

### A. Investigative Methodology
Schlep operated an investigative methodology focused on identifying adult predators targeting minors in high-traffic social experiences (e.g., *Brookhaven RP*, *Adopt Me!*, *Berry Avenue*):
1. **Passive Decoy Monitoring:** Created minor-presenting accounts to observe public chatrooms and spatial voice servers.
2. **Documenting Unsolicited Inbound Lures:** Recorded predators initiating contact, offering Robux bribes, and deploying off-platform migration techniques (Discord, Snapchat, Telegram).
3. **Chain of Custody & Law Enforcement Hand-Off:** Compiled unedited video recordings, user IDs, timestamped server logs, and IP evidence, delivering full packages to the **FBI**, local **Internet Crimes Against Children (ICAC)** task forces, and municipal police departments.

### B. Confirmed Law Enforcement Outcomes
Unlike online drama channels, Schlep’s investigations operated with direct law enforcement coordination:
* **6 Documented Arrests:** Verified law enforcement warrants and criminal indictments across Texas, Florida, and the Midwest directly credited evidence gathered from Schlep's sting operations.
* **Failure of Internal Moderation:** In several instances, the arrested predators had operated active Roblox accounts for months or years, with dozens of unaddressed user report flags in Roblox's internal queue prior to Schlep's external intervention.

## 4. The Sudden Terms of Service Rewrite: The "Anti-Vigilante" Clause

Facing unprecedented public outrage, a viral boycott movement (`#FreeSchlep`, `#StandWithSchlep`), and a Change.org petition exceeding **100,000 verified signatures**, Roblox realized that its existing Terms of Use did not explicitly prohibit collecting evidence of criminal child exploitation for law enforcement.

Within days of the ban, Roblox quietly deployed an **emergency, unannounced rewrite of its Community Standards and Terms of Use**:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                     THE SUDDEN "ANTI-VIGILANTE" TOS AMENDMENT                           │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                                                                                         │
│  "4. SAFETY & PLATFORM INTEGRITY — PROHIBITION ON INDEPENDENT INVESTIGATIONS:           │
│                                                                                         │
│   Users are strictly prohibited from engaging in unauthorized, independent              │
│   investigations, 'vigilante' operations, or 'sting' activities on the platform.        │
│                                                                                         │
│   Specifically, you may not:                                                            │
│   (a) Create decoy, deceptive, or undercover accounts to solicit, bait, or encourage   │
│       policy-violating or harmful behavior from other users;                            │
│   (b) Engage in simulated child endangerment, grooming scenarios, or roleplay involving │
│       inappropriate themes, regardless of intent or purpose;                            │
│   (c) Record, capture, publish, or publicly broadcast interactions with other users    │
│       for the purpose of public exposure, shaming, or third-party investigations;       │
│   (d) Circumvent our official reporting mechanisms by conducting independent           │
│       enforcement operations."                                                          │
│                                                                                         │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### A. Forensic Analysis of the Sudden Clause
1. **The Catch-22 Trap:** By making the creation of decoy accounts and the recording of predatory chats a direct TOS violation, Roblox created a legal architecture where **it is impossible to document platform failures without violating platform rules**.
2. **Inverting the Victim and the Offender:** Roblox corporate PR issued statements claiming Schlep was banned because he *"engaged in simulated grooming conversations that endangered minors on the platform."* This gaslighting tactic transformed the whistleblower who caught 6 predators into an alleged platform safety threat.
3. **Gagging Independent Journalism:** Clause (c) directly targeted investigative journalists, content creators, and independent child safety researchers, prohibiting anyone from publishing video evidence of predators on Roblox under threat of immediate permanent hardware/IP bans.
