## 2. The Steak Utah State Capitol Demonstration (August 15, 2025)

The permanent banning and Cease-and-Desist issued to 22-year-old predator hunter **Schlep** triggered the first major, coordinated physical demonstration organized by top gaming creators:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                 THE STEAK UTAH STATE CAPITOL DEMONSTRATION (AUGUST 2025)                │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ • Organizer / Leader : Popular gaming streamer Steak (millions of followers).           │
│ • Date & Location    : August 15, 2025 at the Utah State Capitol (Salt Lake City, UT).  │
│ • Format             : In-person civic demonstration live-streamed to tens of thousands │
│                        of concurrent viewers on YouTube & Twitch.                       │
│ • Slogans & Banners  : "Free Schlep", "Protect Kids, Not Predators", "Boycott Roblox". │
│ • Demands            : Unconditional unban of Schlep, revocation of the C&D, formal     │
│                        oversight of Trust & Safety, and executive accountability.       │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

* **Physical Mobilization at State Capitol:** Streamer **Steak** gathered content creators, teen players, and parents on the steps of the Utah State Capitol building. Demonstrators held large printed placards displaying chat evidence of predators and slogans condemning Roblox's corporate retaliation.
* **Mass Creator Boycott:** The protest sparked an immediate walkout from the **Roblox Video Stars Program**, where top influencers publicly resigned their creator partnerships.
* **Legislative Ripple Effect:** The live-streamed protest garnered millions of cumulative views, prompting U.S. Representative **Ro Khanna** and state lawmakers to publicly question Roblox’s safety priorities.

## 3. The Chris Hansen CrimeCon Live Demonstration & Ban (August 2026)

At the CrimeCon 2026 national kriminology and investigative conference, veteran television journalist **Chris Hansen** (*To Catch a Predator*, *TruBlu*) joined forces with Schlep for a live investigative presentation:

* **Live Arena Demonstration:** Before an audience of thousands of law enforcement officers, prosecutors, child protection advocates, and journalists, Hansen created a live, unverified minor avatar on Roblox.
* **The Live Ban:** Within minutes of demonstrating how predators initiate contact via unmonitored roleplay lobbies, Roblox's automated threat systems identified the account and **banned Chris Hansen live on the arena projection screens**.
* **Global Media Repercussions:** The live ban proved Hansen and Schlep’s core thesis: Roblox's systems actively prioritize detecting and silencing investigators over capturing active predators.

## 4. Capitol Hill Parent & Survivor Rallies (Washington D.C., 2024–2026)

Organized by child advocacy groups including **Fairplay**, the **National Center on Sexual Exploitation (NCOSE)**, and the **Social Media Victims Law Center (SMVLC)**:

* **The Bereaved Families March:** Parents whose children were groomed, sexually extorted, or driven to suicide through online platforms held demonstrations outside the Russell Senate Office Building and U.S. Capitol steps.
* **Direct Testimony to Senate Judiciary:** During bipartisan hearings led by Senators **Josh Hawley** (R-MO) and **Dick Durbin** (D-IL), families presented photo exhibits of young victims who were trafficked or groomed via Roblox, demanding the passage of the **Kids Online Safety Act (KOSA)** and the revocation of Section 230 platform immunity.

## 5. Developer Labor Actions & Disruptions at RDC

Physical developer conferences have repeatedly served as flashpoints for developer labor discontent:

* **RDC 2021 Security Evacuation (San Francisco):** The conference was disrupted following a security incident and bomb threat hoax, which Roblox corporate counsel subsequently used to file a $1.6M federal SLAPP lawsuit against critic Ruben Sim.
* **RDC 2024–2025 Labor & Unionization Flyering:** Independent developers and labor organizers distributed physical leaflets and pamphlets outside conference halls highlighting studio sweatshop conditions (#SonicSweatshopSimulator / Gamefam), unfair DevEx 75.5%+ platform take-rates, and the lack of worker compensation protections for minor coders.

---

## 6. International Civic Actions & Turkey's 680-Day Ban (2024–2026)

* **Turkey National Ban Protests (August 2024 – June 2026):** Following the initial ban imposed by the Information and Communication Technologies Authority (BTK) over child exploitation concerns, public debates and civic demonstrations took place across Istanbul and Ankara. 
* **The 680-Day Stand-Off:** The platform remained blocked for 680 days until Roblox agreed to establish a direct legal entity in Turkey, overhaul local child safety filters, and implement verified parental controls, leading to the official lifting of the ban on **June 18, 2026**.
